# Spell Casters' Grimoire

Inside of main.c you'll find the firmware that drives the Grimoire!
Flash this firmware to your STM32, take the game from our sister repository


### What do I need to play?

- An evil friend
- STM32 Nucleo F401-RE board
- STMicroelectronic's software suite (STM32CubeIDE and STM32CubeMX)
- Hosyond 3.2" TFT shield (ILI9341 display with XPT2046 resistive touch), matching pinout

### Start the game

- Tap the red and green squares to calibrate your wand
- Draw an element's sigil in **one** stroke
- Draw the enclosing circle seal in **one** stroke
- Cast spells as long as you've got mana

### Spell Sigils to draw:

- Fire  - Triangle
- Water - Horizontal Line
- Lightning - Vertical Line
- Wind - Wave

### How to play

As the spellcaster your job is to RUIN boy's life (your friend) by drawing elemental spells in your grimoire! Make it
impossible for your friend to reach the tower before the time is over. Send gusts of wind lunging them accross the screen, set them on fire, make the
ground slippery, and strike them with lightning.
